Venue Shuttle

Because parking at the venue is extremely limited, a shuttle will be required to get to and from the celebration, departing from the hotel below, 10 minutes from the venue. It will run continuously from 2:50 PM until 4:00 PM, then resume regular trips back starting at 8:00 PM. That said, we know things come up - if you need to head out earlier, just let the shuttle driver know and they'll be happy to take you back sooner.

On site parking can be made available should the shuttle present accessibility barriers, just let us know ahead of time!

Travel Distances

We recommend flying in and out of BWI Marshall Airport as traffic driving out of DC airports can be a challenge.
