Our Story

Gabe and Boni would like to take this time to thank their parents for paying Friends School tuition, because without that investment they might never have met 16 years ago.

What began as a strong friendship capped by a post high school graduation trip to DC together while the rest of their friends were at Bonaroo, naturally grew into what everyone knows today. Through their many phases of life, Boni and Gabe have seen each through it all.

Three dogs and a house later, they are giddy to have you all together to celebrate their lives that led up to this point. And all the life that comes after this with all of you!
